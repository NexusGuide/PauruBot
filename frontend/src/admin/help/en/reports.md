# Sales reports and exports
<!-- screens: /reports -->

[Sales reports](/reports) shows sales for any period and lets you take the data out for accounting. The default period is the last 30 days and up to one year can be chosen.

## Charts and tables

- **Daily sales**: orders and amount per day.
- **By plan**: which plans sold most, split into purchases, renewals and extras.
- **By payment method**: the share of card transfer, gateways and crypto.
- **Top customers**: who bought the most in the period.
- **Sales funnel**: of the people who started the bot in the period, how many shared a phone, took a trial, made a first purchase and bought again; with each step as a share of the one before, and how many days it took half of the buyers to buy. A big drop between two steps shows where to improve.

Resellers see this page only for their own customers.

## Excel export

Under **Excel export**, download orders, payments, services or customers for the period chosen above. Files open in Excel and Google Sheets, and text starting with characters such as = is stored safely.

## Gateway settlements

When a payment method's money (a gateway or a card, say) reaches your bank, record it under **Gateway settlements** with **Record settlement**, including the bank reference. That keeps each method's outstanding amount right and your books matching the bank.

## Daily report

Every day at the hour set with **Daily report hour** (Tehran time) in the **Reports** group of [Settings](/settings), a summary of the day goes to the **Daily report** channel in [Channels](/channels): sales count and amount, renewals, new customers, trials, active and expiring services, pending receipts, open tickets, panels down and the total of all wallet balances.

## History and retention

**Keep logs (days)** in the same group sets how long finished jobs stay in the [Job queue](/jobs). Wallet history, orders and the [Audit log](/audit) are never deleted.
