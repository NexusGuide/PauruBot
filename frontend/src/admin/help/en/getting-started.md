# Getting started
<!-- screens: / /settings -->

After installing, a few short steps make the bot ready to sell. This page gives the suggested order and links to the full guide for each step.

## 1. Connect a panel

In [Panels](/panels) tap **New panel**, pick the panel type (PasarGuard, Marzban, Marzneshin, Remnawave, Rebecca, Guard, Hiddify or 3x-ui), enter its address, username and password, then tap **Test connection**. No service is created until the connection is healthy. Details in [the panels guide](help:panels).

## 2. Create a plan

In [Plans](/catalog) first add a location, then tap **New plan**: name, data, days and price. If you don't pick a panel for the plan, services go to the least loaded panel in its location. More in [the plans guide](help:catalog).

## 3. Turn on a payment method

In [Settings](/settings), **Payments** group, the simplest start is **Card transfer**: add your card numbers under **Cards**. Zarinpal, Telegram Stars and crypto are there too. More in [the payments guide](help:payments).

## 4. Make a test purchase yourself

Open the bot in Telegram, top up the wallet, approve the receipt in [Payments](/payments) and buy a plan. Then check that the service was created on the panel and the subscription link works. If something gets stuck, [System health](/health) usually shows why.

## 5. Set up the look and texts

- In [Bot menu](/bot-menu) arrange the buttons and colours, help topics and app download links.
- In [Bot texts](/texts) you can change any message the bot sends.
- In [Channels](/channels) set report channels, required channels and support contacts.

## 6. Add your staff

In [Staff](/staff) create a separate account with a suitable role for each person; never share the owner's password. More in [the staff and security guide](help:staff-security).

> Before real sales start, make sure in [Backups](/backups) that a daily backup is written, and keep the backup passphrase somewhere off the server.

## Other important settings

[Settings](/settings) is grouped: General, Free trial, Referrals, Payments, Crypto, Services, Custom purchase, Resellers and Reports. Each option has a short hint underneath. Turning off **Bot is on** leaves the bot usable only by staff, which is handy for maintenance and big changes.
